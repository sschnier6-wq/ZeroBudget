How to download your Capital One CSV

1. Open capitalone.com on a desktop computer (mobile app cannot export CSV).

2. Sign in and click your credit card (or bank account).

3. Go to Activity or Transactions.

4. Click Download Transactions (or the download icon).

5. Choose a date range (max ~90 days at a time) and select CSV.

6. Download the file, then come back here and tap “Import Capital One CSV”.

Tip: Large “AUTOPAY” payments are ignored so they don’t inflate your income. Refunds and rewards are imported as income.
